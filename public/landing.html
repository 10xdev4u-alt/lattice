// WHY IT EXISTS

Every research AI gives you an answer.
None of them show their work.

01 · THE PAGE IS THE AUDIT LOG

Watch every claim become traceable

Every tool call — args, result, duration — lands in a log you can reopen, replay, and branch. Export it as a PRISMA-style methods appendix and drop it straight into your thesis. Your committee asks how you know; you hand them the file.

02 · TYPED TOOLS, NOT A CHAT BOX

14 tools with schemas

Every tool carries a JSON-Schema input and read/write annotations. The agent can't guess — it can only call what you've declared, and writes ask permission first.

03 · TWO AGENTS, ONE PAGE

Peer-review cage match

Invite a skeptic persona. It challenges every claim, demands citations, never writes. You watch them negotiate — and you have the transcript.

04 · YOUR MACHINE, YOUR MODEL

Private offline engine

Phi-3-mini runs in your GPU via WebLLM. When the network dies, Lattice keeps answering — papers never leave the machine. 2.1GB, cached once.

05 · FULL TEXT, NOT ABSTRACTS

Drop a PDF, get an index

Drag any PDF in. Two-column read order is reconstructed, a BM25+Porter index is built, and every answer arrives with page-cited evidence chips.

06 · NO LOCK-IN

Open source · Apache 2.0 · one container

Vanilla TypeScript, one Node process, a 57MB Docker artifact. The source, the research briefs, the issue trail — all public. A junior dev can rebuild this from first principles.
